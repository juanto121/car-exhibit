Report (abridged)

TECHNIQUE Chest CT; contrast use not provided.

FINDINGS Lungs: solitary 7 mm solid nodule, smooth margins, right lower lobe. Other structures: not described in the request.

IMPRESSION Incidental 7 mm solid nodule on right lower lobe, low risk.

RECOMMENDATION Non-contrast CT in 6–12 months, consider 18–24 months.

Which segment? · Any calcification or fat? · Is 7 mm the mean of long and short axes? · Other risk factors?

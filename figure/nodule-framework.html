Define the task and format

Prompting
What is added
  • Role: thoracic radiologist
  • Format: Findings / Impression / Recommendation
  • Rule: use only supplied findings; flag missing data
LLM output

FINDINGS Single 7 mm solid nodule, right lower lobe, smooth margins. No prior CT for comparison.

IMPRESSION Indeterminate 7 mm solid pulmonary nodule.

RECOMMENDATION Follow-up CT in 3–6 months (Fleischner).

  • Interval recalled from memory: 3–6 months applies to multiple or subsolid nodules
  • No applicability check or risk category

What is added

LLM output

FINDINGS As in step 1.

IMPRESSION Incidental 7 mm solid nodule. Fleischner applies: age ≥35, not screening, no known cancer, not immunocompromised. Low risk: never smoker, no other risk factors supplied.

RECOMMENDATION CT at 6–12 months, then consider CT at 18–24 months [Fleischner 2017].

  • Guideline-grounded and cited
  • The model still derives the interval itself, so wording can vary between runs
